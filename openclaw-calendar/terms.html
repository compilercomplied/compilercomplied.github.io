OpenClaw Calendar terms of use

Darío Gullón operates OpenClaw Calendar as a personal integration for himself and people he personally invites to his private assistant. The integration reads Calendar information after the Google account holder grants access. Access to the assistant is by invitation.

Calendar answers may be incomplete or outdated. Check Google Calendar directly before relying on an answer for an important appointment.

You can withdraw access at any time in Google Account connections. See the privacy notice for information about data handling and deletion.
