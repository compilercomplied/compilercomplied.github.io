OpenClaw Calendar

OpenClaw Calendar privacy notice

Data the integration accesses

Google provides basic account identity information to identify the authorized account. Read-only Calendar access lets the assistant retrieve calendar and event information, which can include calendar names, event titles, descriptions, times, locations, and attendees.

How it uses and shares data

The integration retrieves Calendar information to respond to requests from people with access to the private assistant. OpenClaw may send relevant Calendar content and the question to OpenAI to generate a response. Google user data is used to provide the requested Calendar features. It is never sold, used for advertising, or used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and protection

The private OpenClaw Gateway stores Google OAuth credentials in an encrypted keyring. Calendar information used in a response may be retained in the assistant’s session history and backups until those records are removed or expire under the operator’s retention settings.

Revocation and deletion

You can revoke access from your Google Account connections. Revocation ends future access. Existing conversation history, local credentials, and backups can be removed separately by the operator. For questions or deletion requests, use the support contact shown on the Google consent screen.

The integration’s use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the Google Workspace user data and developer policy.
